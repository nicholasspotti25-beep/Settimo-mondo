Owned by the weapons stream. Data: data/weapons/*.yaml (38), data/movesets/*.yaml (38 types), data/transitions/*.yaml
(49 ordered category pairs), data/projectiles/weapons.yaml. Sim: src/sim/player.ts (swings), src/sim/arts.ts (Arts).

## Damage model

- Weapon damage = 18 + 4 x D of the source boss (the Lama della Culla: 18). `src/core/formulas.ts weaponDamage`.
- A swing deals weapon damage x `damage_mult` (x `hits` ticks for multi-hit swings); projectile swings deal it per
  projectile. Light/running hits give the light Equilibrio damage, heavy/jumping the heavy one (data/balance.yaml).
- An Art's `damage_mult` is its total DIRECT damage as a multiple of weapon damage: every strike, projectile, explosion
  and secondary wave of the Art landing on one large target adds up to it (`final_mult` weights the last strike,
  `*_share` splits secondary components). `dps_mult` (zones, pits, poison) is extra lingering damage per second while the
  target stays inside. Arte Prima costs 100 Eco and deals +25 Equilibrio, Arte Seconda 200 Eco and +45 (+ `poise_bonus`
  for Abbattimento, Potatura, Punta di Cristallo), applied once on the first connecting hit.
- Transizione: mean of both weapons' damage x the transition's `damage_mult`, Equilibrio `transizione`.

## Roles

Fast (spada corta, pugnali, pugni, artigli, kama, asce gemelle, stocco): short reach, the highest light DPS per point of
weapon damage, high stamina drain. Balanced (spada lunga, katana, scimitarra, spada bastarda; parry-capable). Heavy
(spadone, ascia a due mani, martello, mazza, cesoie): slow wind-ups, the best heavy hits and Equilibrio. Reach
(lancia, alabarda, naginata, tridente, falce, flexible weapons): keep the boss at the tip, lower DPS. Ranged (bows,
crossbow, harpoon, chakram) and magic (catalysts): the lowest close-range DPS, damage from safety, homing projectiles.
Defensive (scudo): bash + absorption 1.0 + parry. No weapon dominates: higher light DPS always costs reach, stamina or
safety, and the late weapons' raw damage is matched to their bosses' HP (TTK target 120 + 20 x D s).
